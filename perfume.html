The Olfactory Pyramid

Top Notes

2

Heart Notes

The core soul of the scent lasting 3-5 hours. Rich florals, intense aromatics, and subtle fruits that balance the profile.

3

Base Notes

The lingering memory lasting up to 24 hours. Deep woods, rare resins, amber, and musk that anchor the fragrance profile.
